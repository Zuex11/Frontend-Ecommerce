Policies

Returns

Items can be returned within 14 days of delivery if unworn and in original condition. Refunds are issued once the item is received back.

Payment

Cash on delivery only. No online payment is required to place an order.

Order cancellation

You can cancel an order yourself while it's pending or in progress. Once it ships, cancellation isn't available.

Privacy

We only use your information to process and deliver your orders. No email is required to sign up.
